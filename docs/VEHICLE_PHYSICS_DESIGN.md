## 1. Architectural Philosophy

The vehicle physics overhaul replaces the previous `CharacterBody3D` kinematic approximation with a **100% Data-Driven RigidBody3D Simulation**.

### Core Tenet: Zero Physics Code Touched for Vehicle Operations
Adding, removing, or swapping a car is strictly an **asset/data operation** (`.tres` resource + `.tscn` visual prefab). The physics core (`vehicles/core/`) contains **zero car names, zero asset paths, and zero model-specific magic numbers**.

## 2. Key Architecture Components

### 2.1 `VehicleConfig` (`res://vehicles/core/VehicleConfig.gd`)
A custom `Resource` encapsulating the complete mathematical definition of a vehicle:
- **Mass & Inertia**: `mass` ($kg$), `center_of_mass_offset` ($m$), `moment_of_inertia_scale`.
- **Dimensions**: `wheelbase` ($m$), `track_width_front` ($m$), `track_width_rear` ($m$), `chassis_size` ($m$).
- **Powertrain**: `peak_power_kw` ($kW$), `max_engine_rpm`, `gear_ratios` (Array of floats), `final_drive_ratio`, `drivetrain_type` (FWD / RWD / AWD).
- **Steering**: `max_steer_angle_deg`, `min_steer_angle_deg`, `steer_speed`, `return_speed`, `ackermann_factor`.
- **Suspension**: `suspension_rest_length` ($m$), `suspension_travel` ($m$), `spring_stiffness_front/rear` ($N/m$), `bump_damping_front/rear` ($N\cdot s/m$), `rebound_damping_front/rear` ($N\cdot s/m$), `anti_roll_front/rear` ($N/m$), `bump_stop_stiffness` ($N/m$).
- **Tire Dynamics**: `tire_longitudinal_friction`, `tire_lateral_friction`, `slip_angle_peak_deg`, `slip_ratio_peak`, `rolling_resistance_coeff`.
- **Aerodynamics**: `drag_coefficient`, `frontal_area` ($m^2$), `downforce_coefficient`.
- **Visuals**: `visual_scene_path`, `visual_scale`, `visual_offset`, `visual_yaw_offset_deg`.
- **Validation**: Built-in `validate() -> Array[String]` ensuring physical sanity before runtime instantiation.

### 2.2 `VehicleVisualAdapter` (`res://vehicles/core/VehicleVisualAdapter.gd`)
A non-intrusive runtime wrapper that:
- Instantiates the visual scene specified by `VehicleConfig.visual_scene_path`.
- Constructs a runtime dual-pivot hierarchy for each wheel:
  - `WheelHub`: Moves vertically along local $Y$ corresponding to physical suspension travel $x$.
  - `SteerPivot`: Rotates around local $Y$ corresponding to Ackermann steering angle $\delta$.
  - `SpinPivot`: Rotates around local $X$ corresponding to wheel angular velocity $\omega$.
- Wraps the visual body mesh in `BodyPivot` to express dynamic roll, pitch, and heave generated by simulated chassis forces.
- Preserves custom player paint tinting without overwriting shared disk materials.

### 2.3 `VehicleCollisionBuilder` (`res://vehicles/core/VehicleCollisionBuilder.gd`)
Procedurally generates a chamfered convex chassis shape (`ConvexPolygonShape3D`) from `VehicleConfig` dimensions:
- Front and rear bottom edges feature upward ski-ramp chamfers.
- Prevents front or rear bumper snagging on coplanar road seams, curb edges, and steep ramp transitions.

### 2.4 `VehicleRegistry` (`res://vehicles/registry/VehicleRegistry.gd`)
Central decoupled catalog:
- Scans `res://vehicles/configs/*.tres` on initialization.
- Maps string IDs to `VehicleConfig`.
- Provides alias resolution for legacy names (`starter_gt` $\to$ `sedan`, `apex_cruiser` $\to$ `coupe`, `phantom_turbo` $\to$ `sport`).
- Safe fallback: Always returns a valid configuration even if an unknown vehicle ID is requested.

## 3. Design Decisions and Mathematical Formulations

### Decision 1: `RigidBody3D` with Raycast/Shapecast Suspension
- **Rationale**: `CharacterBody3D` requires manual kinematic integration and lacks natural momentum, rotational inertia, and collision restitution. By using `RigidBody3D`, gravity acts naturally along slope gradients ($F_{\text{gravity}} = m \cdot g \cdot \sin\theta$).
- **Suspension Force Formulation**:
  $$F_{\text{suspension}} = \max\left(0, k \cdot x + c \cdot v_{\text{rel}} + F_{\text{anti\_roll}} + F_{\text{bump\_stop}}\right)$$
  Where:
  - $x = L_{\text{rest}} - L_{\text{measured}}$ is the spring compression.
  - $v_{\text{rel}}$ is the relative vertical wheel velocity along the suspension axis.
  - $c = c_{\text{bump}}$ when $v_{\text{rel}} > 0$ (compression), and $c = c_{\text{rebound}}$ when $v_{\text{rel}} < 0$ (extension). Rebound damping is calibrated $40\%\text{--}60\%$ stiffer than bump damping to prevent post-jump oscillation.

### Decision 2: Decoupled Anti-Roll Bar (ARB)
- Left and right suspension deflections on the same axle transfer force to counteract chassis roll:
  $$F_{\text{ARB, left}} = k_{\text{ARB}} \cdot (x_{\text{left}} - x_{\text{right}})$$
  $$F_{\text{ARB, right}} = -F_{\text{ARB, left}}$$
- Keeps the chassis flat and responsive in high-speed corners without sacrificing bump compliance over single-wheel road imperfections.

### Decision 3: Procedural Anti-Seam Chamfer Collision Hull
- Rather than using a generic bounding box that catches on road polygon edges, `VehicleCollisionBuilder` lifts the front-most and rear-most bottom vertices by $+0.16$ m and $+0.12$ m respectively, creating a smooth approach ramp.

### Decision 4: Analytical Ride Height Calibration & Dynamic Ground Contact Clamping
- **Hover Bug Diagnosis**: 3D vehicle models are authored with ground contact at $Y = 0$, while a `RigidBody3D` with raycast suspension floats its physical origin at suspension equilibrium height ($H_{\text{chassis}} \approx 0.466$ m above road). Without vertical calibration, visual meshes floated $\approx 50$ cm above the road.
- **Analytical Vertical Offset**:
  $$\Delta y = (y_{\text{mount}} - (L_{\text{rest}} - c_{\text{eq}})) - \bar{y}_{\text{model\_wheel}}$$
  Applied automatically to `visual_root.position.y` at initialization, seating the car body naturally onto the suspension.
- **Dynamic Wheel Ground Clamping**:
  When a wheel raycast reports ground contact, the wheel hub position is dynamically clamped to the road surface:
  $$\mathbf{p}_{\text{hub}} = \mathbf{p}_{\text{contact}} + \mathbf{n}_{\text{contact}} \cdot R_{\text{wheel}}$$
  converted to chassis local coordinates while preserving local lateral ($X$) and longitudinal ($Z$) alignment inside the wheel wells. When airborne, the wheel smoothly drops to full suspension extension under gravity.

### Decision 5: Explosion-Proof Suspension & Wall Normal Filtering
- **Catapult Root Cause**: When grazing concrete barriers or curbs, raycasts previously registered steep barrier faces as drivable road (`hit.normal.dot(chassis_up) > 0.1`). The lateral barrier normal and sudden single-frame penetration spike produced massive upward/lateral forces exceeding $100,000$ N, catapulting the car.
- **Surface Filter**: Raycast hits are rejected if $\mathbf{n}_{\text{hit}} \cdot \mathbf{UP} \le 0.55$ or $\mathbf{n}_{\text{hit}} \cdot \mathbf{chassis\_up} \le 0.45$, treating barriers strictly as solid rigid body collisions.
- **Strut Axis Force Application**: Suspension forces are applied strictly along `chassis_up` (the mechanical strut axis) rather than tilted surface normals, eliminating lateral roll moments.
- **Safety Ceiling**: Corner spring force is capped at $2.8 \times \frac{m \cdot g}{4}$, and suspension compression velocity is clamped to $[-4.0, 4.0]$ m/s, mathematically preventing physics explosions.

### Decision 6: Anti-Wall-Climb Procedural Chassis Hull
- In [vehicles/core/VehicleCollisionBuilder.gd](file:///c:/Users/Pongo/OneDrive/Documents/racing/vehicles/core/VehicleCollisionBuilder.gd), the chassis collision shape width is proceduralized to match or slightly exceed outer wheel track width:
  $$w_{\text{outer}} = \frac{1}{2}\max(T_{\text{front}}, T_{\text{rear}}) + \frac{1}{2}W_{\text{wheel}}$$
- Solid vertical side rub-rails prevent wheel rims from catching or climbing slanted concrete barrier bases during wall scrapes.

### Decision 7: Automatic Slope Hold (Hill Idle Brake)
- When throttle and brake inputs are $\le 0.05$ and forward speed $< 2.0$ KM/H, full holding brake torque is applied automatically across all grounded wheels.
- At near standstill ($v < 0.1$ m/s), residual velocity damping cancels gravity drift, anchoring the vehicle completely at $0$ KM/H on slopes.

### Decision 8: Intelligent Multi-Stage Auto-Unflip System
- Monitored continuously in `_physics_process`:
  - If $\mathbf{chassis\_up} \cdot \mathbf{UP} < 0.35$ (vehicle rolled $> 69^\circ$ or inverted) and speed $< 3.0$ m/s for $\ge 2.0$ s:
  - Zeros out linear and angular velocities.
  - Retains horizontal forward yaw while removing pitch and roll.
  - Probes ground height via downward raycast and drops vehicle safely from $+1.35$ m.
  - Performs lateral obstacle clearance scans to nudge vehicle away from adjacent barrier walls into lane center.
  - Resets all internal wheel compressions and engages a $2.5$ s cooldown.
